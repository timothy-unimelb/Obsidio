3 · the door

A quick no beats a long wait

beer · /pricesalad · /statspizza · /riskpast its latency barturned away (503)
Now a rush: more pizza orders than two ovens can bake. Parked customers earn nothing, and the oldest order will blow its deadline even if served now. So: newest first, stale ones skipped, and when both ovens are busy and someone is already waiting, a new pizza order is turned away at the door in a millisecond — inside an error budget of 0.88% against the 1% gate. That customer comes straight back and orders beers; the moment an oven frees, the next pizza goes in.
4 · interleaving

Four chains, interleaved

All hashing work together+84%1,953,954 → 3,598,675 on the x86 reference, exact grader
A single SHA-256 chain is strictly serial: each step needs the previous result, so the core sits idle waiting. Run four independent chains and interleave their instructions — while chain A waits on its result, chain B hashes, then C, then D — and the waits overlap. Taking all the hashing work together we raised the score by around 80%.
